LEARNING PATH 01

Stock market: start with the basics

Learn how ownership in a company works before choosing a share.

  • Understand shares, dividends, market orders, and limit orders.
  • Read the company's financial statements and compare valuation measures.
  • Check brokerage charges, taxes, liquidity, and concentration risk.
  • Write down why you are buying, what could change your view, and how long you plan to hold.

Mentor question: Can you explain how the company earns money and what could make its profit fall?

Frequently asked questions

Know before you invest.

Can an MMF guarantee my money?

No. MMFs are generally designed for lower-risk short-term investing, but they are not bank deposits and returns or capital are not guaranteed. Read the fund's current documents and risk disclosures.

Can this website tell me exactly what to buy?

This website provides general education and calculators, not personalised financial advice or live market signals. Consider your goals, financial position, risk tolerance, and licensed professional advice where appropriate.

Does this website automatically show adverts?

No. The advert area lets you draft and preview ad copy in this demo. Live ads need a hosting setup and an ad network or a connected form/backend.

How do I make this website appear in Google?

Publish it on a real domain, use Search Console, create original helpful articles, keep information updated, make pages fast and mobile-friendly, and earn genuine relevant links. No one can guarantee a viral result or a top search ranking.

Protect your money

Scam detection and verification centre

Pause before sending money. Scammers often copy real company names, logos, staff profiles and regulator certificates.

🚩

Warning signs

Guaranteed profits, pressure to act immediately, referral rewards that depend on recruitment, secret signals, or requests to pay a personal wallet are major red flags.

🔎

Verify independently

Open the regulator's official website yourself. Check the exact legal name, licence category, domain and contact details. A logo or screenshot is not proof.

Check CMA resources ↗

🛑

If something feels wrong

Do not send more money to “unlock” a withdrawal. Save messages, receipts, account details and website links; contact your bank or payment provider and relevant authorities promptly.

Five questions before investing

  1. Can I independently verify this company and the exact product?
  2. Do I understand how it earns returns and what can go wrong?
  3. Are the fees, withdrawal rules and legal documents clear?
  4. Am I being promised a guaranteed or unusually high return?
  5. Can I afford a loss without affecting rent, food, school fees or emergency needs?

This checklist is educational and does not replace a regulator's current records or professional advice.
